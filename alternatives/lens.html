Lens alternative

A Kubernetes desktop client without a Lens account

Lens is the well-known Kubernetes IDE. If you want the same kind of day-to-day GUI — pods, logs, YAML, exec, Helm — but you do not want a vendor account or cluster credentials leaving your machine, KubeSpade is built for that.

Teams look for a Lens alternative after the product moved to a freemium model, after OpenLens stopped being a viable fork, or because they simply want a client that talks to the API the way kubectl does: from your laptop, with your kubeconfig, no extra login.

KubeSpade is an independent Kubernetes GUI for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows (x64 and ARM), and Linux (.deb and AppImage). Connect with a kubeconfig, Amazon EKS, Google GKE, or Azure AKS. There is no KubeSpade cloud that receives your tokens.

When Lens is the better fit

  • You depend on a specific Lens extension.
  • Your org standardizes on Lens Pro / Spaces.
  • You need the deepest Prometheus-in-IDE setup Lens already has.

Those are real advantages. KubeSpade does not pretend to be a Lens clone with a larger plugin store.

When people switch

  • They do not want another account in the cluster path.
  • OpenLens is no longer a maintained escape hatch.
  • They want a GUI that stays on the device, including later on iPad.
Is KubeSpade a drop-in Lens replacement?

For browsing clusters, logs, YAML, exec, and Helm — yes, that is the job. It is not a drop-in for the Lens extension ecosystem or Lens team features.

Do I need kubectl installed?

Not for clusters you connect in the app. The desktop client talks to the Kubernetes API itself. You can still use kubectl alongside it for scripts.

Is there a free download?

Yes. Desktop builds are on the Download page (GitHub Releases, with SHA256SUMS). Verify the checksum before you install.
