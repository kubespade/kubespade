Privacy Policy

KubeSpade · Last updated: August 10, 2026

KubeSpade (“the App”) is a Kubernetes management client for iPad (and related desktop apps). This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we store on your device

Cluster credentials, kubeconfig material, cloud keys, and session tokens are stored only on your device (iOS Keychain / secure storage). We do not operate a backend that receives your cluster credentials.

Network

The App connects directly from your device to Kubernetes API servers and cloud provider APIs that you configure. Traffic does not pass through our servers.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment data under Apple’s privacy policy. We receive entitlement status from StoreKit on device; we do not receive your payment card details.

Analytics & tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across apps. Optional on-device debug logs stay on your device unless you export and share them.

Children

The App is intended for professional use and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support email listed on the App Store product page.
